White-label and license pages

The pages under /white-label and /verify-license ask our licensing service for prices, quotes and license checks, straight from your browser, so your IP address and the request reach it. When you send a form there, it receives what you typed: your name, email, company, npm scope, docs domain and message. When online payment is open, continuing to pay takes you to the payment provider's own checkout page.

For a license we keep your name, email, company, domain, order and license key, to deliver the build, answer verification checks and send renewal notices. A public verification shows only the client name, the licensed domains, the license type, its status and its dates, never your email or the price.
