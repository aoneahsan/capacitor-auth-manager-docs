Updates and renewals

An update is a release of capacitor-auth-manager, rebuilt with your rename and published under your scope. Your license includes … months of them.

What counts as an update

  • A patch, like 3.1.1: fixes, same API.
  • A feature release, like 3.2.0, which added Google's rendered button flow.
  • A major, like 3.0.0, published with its migration notes on your docs site.
  • A provider enabled upstream, once it's verified there, reaches your build in the next release.

The included months run from the purchase date. Without a renewal, the versions already published under your name stay on npm and keep working. New releases stop until you renew.
