# Updates and renewals

Each release of `capacitor-auth-manager` is rebuilt with your rename and published under your scope, with its changelog entry on your docs site. That covers patches, feature releases, majors with their migration notes, and providers as they're enabled upstream.

Updates are included for a set period from the purchase date. After that, a renewal of 3, 6 or 12 months continues them, counted from the later of the renewal date and your current end date, so renewing early loses nothing.

If updates lapse, the versions already published under your name stay on npm and keep working. New releases stop until you renew.

Current renewal prices, and the form to renew with your license key, are on the [updates page](/white-label/updates).
