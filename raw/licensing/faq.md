# White-label questions

## Do we get the source code?

It's already public under MIT on [GitHub](https://github.com/aoneahsan/capacitor-auth-manager). The license covers our work on your build and the right to use the Capacitor Auth Manager name and logo.

## Couldn't we just fork it?

Yes, MIT allows that. A license makes sense when you'd rather not maintain the fork: the rename across npm, iOS and Android on every release, Google SDK changes, and the docs.

## Which providers does our build include?

The same as the upstream release it's built from. In 3.x that's Google on web, iOS and Android.

## Is the license exclusive?

No. We may license the package to other teams, and there's no buyout of open code.

## What happens if we stop renewing?

The versions already published under your name stay on npm and keep working. New releases stop.

## Can you change the package for us?

Yes, as [custom work](/white-label/custom-work), quoted separately from the license.
